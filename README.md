# MyMec

**Your garage's maintenance, on your phone.** MyMec keeps every vehicle's
service plan, odometer, and work history in one place and reminds you before
something is due. It works fully offline with no account, no ads, and no
tracking.

## Features

- **A service plan for every vehicle.** Pick the make and model and MyMec
  sets up 16 services (oil, tire rotation, brakes, brake fluid, filters,
  coolant, transmission, spark plugs, belts, differential and transfer case
  fluid, wipers, alignment, and inspections) at the sooner end of common
  intervals. Axle fluids start paused on vehicles that may not have them.
  You can change any interval, add your own services, and delete any
  service you don't need.
- **Knows when things are due.** Each service is due by date or mileage,
  whichever comes first. MyMec learns how much you drive from your odometer
  readings, so it can tell when a service is coming up between readings.
- **Reminders.** Local notifications before a service is due, on the day,
  and weekly while it is overdue. Snooze a reminder, or skip a cycle you do
  not need.
- **Service history.** Log work as you do it or add older records: date,
  mileage, cost, shop or DIY, parts, labor, and notes. History is grouped by
  year, with totals and a 12-month activity view, and filters by service and
  date.
- **PDF service records.** Export a vehicle's history as a PDF to print,
  email, or hand over when you sell. You choose what it includes:
  odometer, cost, shop, notes, parts, totals, and the current service plan,
  and can preview every page before you save or share it.
- **Miles or kilometers,** per vehicle.
- **Backups you control.** Save your whole garage to a `.mymec` file and
  restore it on any device.
- **Optional encrypted cloud sync.** Turn it on to keep devices in sync with
  a 16-digit code. Your garage is encrypted on your phone before it is
  uploaded, so the server cannot read it. No email or password needed.
- **App lock** with Face ID, fingerprint, or your passcode.
- Light and dark themes, using your wallpaper's colors on supported Android
  devices.

## Getting started

1. Open MyMec and tap **Add**. Choose the year, make, and model, then give
   the vehicle a nickname if you like (its look can be changed later from
   **Edit vehicle**).
2. Enter the current odometer reading. Update it every so often (tap the
   pencil on the vehicle's odometer) so due dates stay accurate.
3. Open the vehicle to see its **Services**: what is overdue, due soon, and
   coming up. Tap a service to log it, change its interval, snooze, skip,
   pause, or delete it.
4. Use **History** to review past work, **Add history** for older records,
   and **Export PDF** at the bottom of History for a printable record of
   what the filter shows.
5. In **Settings** (top right of the garage) you can turn on reminders,
   cloud sync, and app lock, change the theme, and back up or restore.

## Privacy

Everything you enter stays on your phone. MyMec has no analytics, no ads, and
no sign-in. Cloud sync is off unless you turn it on, and even then only
encrypted data leaves your device.
